Baby Piano

Tap, slide, or bang on the keys!

  • Song mode: every tap plays the next note of Twinkle Twinkle, Mary Had a Little Lamb, Row Row Row Your Boat, and Old MacDonald. Any key works, so it always sounds like a song.
  • Turn song mode off to play each key's own note.
  • To leave, hold the top-right corner for 2 seconds.
  • iPad or iPhone: turn on Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility) so little hands can't swipe out.
