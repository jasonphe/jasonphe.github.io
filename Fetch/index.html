Fetch!

Throw it, and the puppy brings it back!

Pick a puppy

  • Tap anywhere to throw a toy there. Tap high in the sky for a big throw.
  • Tap the puppy and it does a trick: speak, roll over, spin or jump.
  • Every 5 fetches the puppy runs off to a new place: the park, the beach, the snow and the moon.
  • To leave, hold the top-right corner for 2 seconds.
  • Computer: click, or press any key to throw.
