Choo-Choo Train

All aboard!

  • The big green button makes the train go, and red makes it stop. The horn button toots the whistle.
  • The train stops at every station so animal friends can hop on and off.
  • Tap the train, the riders, or the animals in the fields to hear them.
  • To leave, hold the top-right corner for 2 seconds.
  • Computer: Space or Enter means go and stop, and any other key toots.
