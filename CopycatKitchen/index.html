How to cook

  1. Pantry: tap ingredients to put them on the counter.
  2. Board: cut things up. Tap the board to chop.
  3. Bowl: mix things together. Flour, egg and yeast make dough, and dough bakes into bread.
  4. Stove and oven: set the heat and watch the color. Food keeps cooking while you're at another station. Toss the pan to combine what's in it, like a stir-fry.
  5. Plate: plate up, season, and serve. Salt is invisible, so use a taste test to find it.
