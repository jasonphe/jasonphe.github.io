Balloon Float

Hold to blow, let go to float!

  • Hold a finger down to blow up a balloon. Let go and it floats away.
  • Hold too long and it goes pop! Tap floating balloons to pop them too. Some have a friend inside.
  • To leave, hold the top-right corner for 2 seconds.
  • Computer: hold the mouse button, or hold down any key.
