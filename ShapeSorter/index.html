Shape Sorter

Put each shape in its hole!

  • Drag a shape into the matching hole. Tap a shape to hear its name.
  • Extra help: drop a shape anywhere on the box and it finds its own hole.
  • Each finished box adds a new shape, up to five.
  • To leave, hold the top-right corner for 2 seconds.
  • Computer: it goes full screen, and any key pops the next shape into its hole.
