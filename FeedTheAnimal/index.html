Feed the Animal

Somebody is hungry!

  • Drag food into the open mouth. Tap food to hear its name, and tap the critter to tickle it.
  • Asks for food: a thought bubble shows what it wants. Any food is fine, but the right one gets hearts.
  • After five bites it's full, and a new hungry friend comes along.
  • To leave, hold the top-right corner for 2 seconds.
  • Computer: it goes full screen, and any key feeds it a snack.
