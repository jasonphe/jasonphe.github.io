Where's the Ball?

Bounce it, then find where it hides!

Hiding spots
  • Tap the ball to bounce it. Every so often it hides behind something. Start with 1 or 2 hiding spots and add more as she gets the hang of it. The spot wiggles and peeks to help, and pops out by itself if nobody finds it.
  • To leave, hold the top-right corner for 2 seconds.
  • iPad or iPhone: turn on Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility) so little hands can't swipe out.
